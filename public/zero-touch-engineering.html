01 — The gap I keep seeing

I've talked to a lot of people about AI. The same friction points show up every time.

Everyone wants the leverage. Almost no one has a workflow that holds. The problem is rarely the model — it's the distance between a good idea and something a team can trust, review, and ship.

The interested

People are genuinely interested — but they don't know where to start.

The teams

Teams try the tools — but the workflow never sticks.

The leaders

Leaders want leverage — but they don't want chaos.

The developers

Developers want speed — but not sloppy output they have to babysit.

02 — Zero-touch engineering
The goal is not to remove people from the process. It's to remove the unnecessary touches between intent and a reviewable result.
Humans do

Strategy, planning, judgment, taste, and review — the parts that genuinely require a person.

Agents do

Research, drafting, scaffolding, packaging, status updates, and repeatable execution — grounded in real business context.

The result

You review prepared work instead of dragging every task through the process by hand.

03 — Nine ways to picture it

The mental models I keep coming back to.

01 / METAPHOR

Air Traffic Control

Most teams fly every plane by hand. I build the control tower — humans make the calls, agents handle routing, monitoring, checklists, and handoffs.

02 / METAPHOR

The Review Desk

Less chasing updates across five tools. The system brings you the next best version of the work, context attached. You approve, redirect, or refine.

03 / METAPHOR

The Factory Floor

Not a magic text box off to the side. Specialized stations, clear inputs, quality checks, human supervisors — a visible path from raw idea to finished output.

04 / METAPHOR

The Senior Associate

It knows the context, drafts the work, surfaces risks, and asks when judgment is needed. It doesn't make the human disappear — it makes the human more effective.

05 / METAPHOR

Blank Page → Approval Queue

Most AI still leaves you staring at a blank page. I move teams to approval-queue workflows, where the thinking, drafting, and packaging is done enough for humans to decide.

06 / METAPHOR

The Operating Room

The human is still the surgeon. But the room is prepared, the instruments ready, the vitals monitored. That's the difference between a chatbot and an operating system for work.

07 / METAPHOR

The AI Factory

Set the line running and walk away. Agents take a clear intent and work AFK — researching, building, packaging — so prompt-to-production output is waiting on the belt when you come back to inspect it.

08 / METAPHOR

The Pit Crew

You're the driver making the race calls. The crew is rehearsed, synchronized, and fast — handling the work in the background so every second you spend is on the decisions that win.

09 / METAPHOR

Self-Driving, With a Wheel

The system can take the whole route on its own. But the wheel never leaves your hands — you can steer, slow down, or take over the instant judgment is required.
